REMOTE WORK POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Owner: Human Resources

1. Domestic Remote Work
Employees may work remotely from any location within their home country
without prior approval, provided they remain reachable during core hours
(10am–3pm in their local team's primary timezone) and maintain a stable
internet connection.

2. International Remote Work
Working remotely from another country requires prior written approval from
both HR and Legal, due to tax residency, payroll, and work-visa
implications. Submit requests at least 3 weeks before travel via the HR
portal's "International Remote Work Request" form.

- Trips of 30 consecutive days or fewer are generally approved if the
  employee's role has no data residency restrictions.
- Trips longer than 30 consecutive days require additional review and may
  affect the employee's tax and benefits status; approval is not guaranteed.
- Employees may not work from a country under active US/EU trade sanctions.

3. Equipment and Security
Remote employees must use company-issued devices with full-disk encryption
and must connect via the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
Working from public Wi-Fi without VPN is not permitted.

4. Fully Remote Roles
Roles explicitly designated "remote-first" in the job description follow
this same policy for international travel, but do not require any approval
for domestic remote work.

5. Manager Discretion
Managers may require specific in-office days for team collaboration,
communicated with at least 2 weeks notice.

Questions: hr@company.example or legal@company.example (for international requests)
