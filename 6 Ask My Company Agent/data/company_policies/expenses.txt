EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Owner: Finance

1. Eligible Expenses
Reasonable, business-related expenses are reimbursable, including travel
(flights, trains, taxis/rideshare), lodging, client meals, conference fees,
and required software subscriptions pre-approved by a manager.

2. Spending Limits (without prior written approval)
- Meals: up to $50/day domestic, $75/day international
- Lodging: up to $200/night domestic, $300/night international
- Ground transportation: up to $100/day
- Any single expense over $500 requires manager pre-approval before purchase

3. Submission
All expenses must be submitted through the Finance portal within 30 days
of being incurred, with an itemized receipt attached. Expenses submitted
after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in exceptional circumstances
approved by Finance leadership.

4. Approval Chain
- Under $500: direct manager approval
- $500–$2,000: department head approval
- Over $2,000: VP or Finance approval required

5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, alcohol beyond one drink with a client meal, traffic
or parking fines, and first-class airfare (unless flight time exceeds 6
hours) are not reimbursable.

6. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expenses are reimbursed via payroll within 2 pay cycles of
submission.
