VACATION POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Owner: Human Resources

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for paid vacation leave starting on
their first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue vacation on a
pro-rated basis according to scheduled hours.

2. Accrual Rates
- 0–2 years of tenure: 15 vacation days per year (1.25 days/month)
- 3–5 years of tenure: 20 vacation days per year (1.67 days/month)
- 6+ years of tenure: 25 vacation days per year (2.08 days/month)

3. Carryover
Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the following
calendar year. Any balance beyond this cap is forfeited on December 31st
unless a manager approves an exception in writing before that date.

4. Requesting Time Off
- Requests longer than 3 consecutive days require at least 2 weeks notice.
- Requests of 1–2 days require at least 3 business days notice.
- All requests are submitted through the HR portal, not by email or chat.

5. Approval
Vacation requests require direct manager approval. Managers must respond
within 3 business days; unanswered requests older than 3 business days are
automatically escalated to the department head.

6. Payout at Termination
Employees are paid out for unused, accrued vacation days upon termination,
in accordance with local labor law.
