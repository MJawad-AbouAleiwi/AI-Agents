SICK LEAVE POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Owner: Human Resources

1. Allotment
All full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year,
credited in full on January 1st (or on the employee's start date, for new
hires joining mid-year, pro-rated by remaining months).

2. Usage
Sick days may be used for the employee's own illness, medical appointments,
or to care for an immediate family member. Sick days do not need to be used
consecutively.

3. Notification
Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the day of
absence, ideally before their normal start time, via chat or email.

4. Documentation
A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive
days. For shorter absences, no documentation is required.

5. Carryover
Unused sick days do not carry over to the next calendar year and are not
paid out upon termination.

6. Extended Illness
Absences expected to exceed 10 sick days should be discussed with HR to
evaluate short-term disability or other leave options.
