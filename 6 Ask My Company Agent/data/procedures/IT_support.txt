IT SUPPORT PROCEDURE
Owner: IT Helpdesk

1. Submitting a Ticket
All IT issues are submitted through the IT Helpdesk portal, not by direct
message to IT staff, so requests can be tracked and prioritized correctly.

2. Priority Levels and Response Times
- Critical (system down, security incident): response within 30 minutes,
  24/7 via the emergency hotline
- High (blocking daily work, e.g. cannot log in): response within 4
  business hours
- Medium (partial functionality issue): response within 1 business day
- Low (feature request, minor annoyance): response within 3 business days

3. Common Self-Service Options
Password resets, VPN reconnects, and software installs from the approved
catalog can be completed instantly through the IT Self-Service Portal
without opening a ticket.

4. Hardware Issues
For a malfunctioning company device, open a ticket with "Hardware" as the
category. IT will arrange a loaner device within 1 business day for High
or Critical priority hardware failures.

5. After-Hours Support
Only Critical issues are supported after standard business hours (9am–6pm,
Mon–Fri). Use the emergency hotline listed on the IT intranet page.

6. Escalation
If a ticket has not been addressed within its target response time, reply
to the ticket with "ESCALATE" or contact the IT manager directly.
