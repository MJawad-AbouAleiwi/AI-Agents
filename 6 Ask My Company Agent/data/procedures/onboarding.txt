NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING PROCEDURE
Owner: People Operations

Step 1 — Before Day One
IT provisions a laptop, email account, and Slack/Teams access at least 2
business days before the employee's start date. HR sends the offer packet,
benefits enrollment link, and I-9/tax forms for the employee to complete.

Step 2 — Day One
- 9:00am: Welcome meeting with manager
- 10:00am: IT setup session (device, accounts, VPN, MFA enrollment)
- 11:00am: HR orientation (benefits, payroll, policy overview)
- Afternoon: Team introductions and initial project walkthrough

Step 3 — First Week
The manager assigns an onboarding buddy and a 30/60/90-day plan. The new
employee completes mandatory compliance training (security awareness,
code of conduct, anti-harassment) within the first 5 business days.

Step 4 — 30-Day Check-in
Manager and new employee meet to review progress against the 30-day plan
and address any blockers.

Step 5 — 90-Day Review
Formal review of performance against the 90-day plan, with HR looped in on
outcomes. This marks the end of the standard probationary period.
