PURCHASING PROCEDURE (EQUIPMENT & SOFTWARE)
Owner: IT & Finance

1. Standard Equipment (laptops, monitors, peripherals)
Employees requesting standard-issue equipment (e.g. a laptop from the
approved model list) submit a request through the IT portal. No separate
Finance approval is needed if the item is on the pre-approved catalog and
under $1,500.

2. Non-Standard or High-Cost Equipment
Requests for equipment not on the approved catalog, or costing over
$1,500, require:
  a. A brief business justification submitted with the IT request
  b. Manager approval
  c. IT review for security/compatibility compliance
  Approval typically takes 3–5 business days.

3. Preferred Vendors
IT purchases hardware through preferred vendors only (see the internal
vendor list) to ensure warranty and asset-tracking coverage. Employees
should not personally purchase equipment for reimbursement except in
approved exceptional cases (see step 4).

4. Personal Purchase + Reimbursement (exceptions only)
If pre-approved by IT and Finance in advance, an employee may buy equipment
personally and submit it via the standard Expense Reimbursement process,
with the itemized receipt and IT's written pre-approval attached.

5. Software Purchases
New software or SaaS subscriptions must go through IT for security review
before purchase, regardless of cost, due to data-handling and licensing
requirements. Submit via the "Software Request" form in the IT portal.

6. Asset Return
All company-purchased equipment must be returned to IT upon offboarding or
role change where the equipment is no longer needed.
